sushruta labs

Sushruta Labs is a digital health platform that uses digital twin technology to connect patients and doctors. Each patient gets a virtual replica built from their health data, including history, vitals, reports and symptoms. Doctors can use it to understand the patient's condition, track changes over time, and plan care without relying on scattered records or repeated in-person visits.
